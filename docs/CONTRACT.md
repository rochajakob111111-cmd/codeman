# Bridge contract (Minecraft client <-> Blade & Sorcery, same PC)

- **Transport:** TCP on `127.0.0.1:bridgePort` (default 25599). Minecraft listens, B&S connects and retries every 2 s.
  Frame = u32 length (big-endian) | u8 message id | fields, big-endian, Java `DataOutputStream` encodings.
  Messages, fields and rates: `sheets/protocol.json` (code generated by `tools/gen.py` on both sides;
  `tests/protocol_roundtrip.py` checks the two agree byte for byte).
- **Protocol version:** number of messages in the sheet; both sides refuse a mismatch with a log line.
- **Units and axes:** 1 Minecraft block = 1 metre. Unity = (x - ox, y - oy + anchorY, oz - z): z flips
  (Minecraft right-handed, Unity left-handed). Yaw: Unity = 180 + Minecraft. Origin (ox, oy, oz) starts at the
  Welcome block and moves whenever the player is 256 blocks from it (floating origin, B&S side only).
- **Who owns what:**
  - Position and look: B&S (the VR body). Minecraft applies poses only when their `teleportSeq` matches its own.
  - Server teleports (respawn, /tp, corrections): Minecraft bumps `teleportSeq`; B&S moves the player there.
  - Health, damage and death of everyone: Minecraft (server). B&S proxies have 100000 local HP and are
    killed (ragdolled) only when Minecraft says so; the VR player's B&S health mirrors Minecraft's.
  - Hits: B&S detects them physically and sends raw B&S damage + type + weapon velocity; the Minecraft
    server scales by `sheets/damage.json`, checks reach (6 blocks, 48 thrown) and a 250 ms cooldown.
- **Back-pressure:** Minecraft sends at most `sectionsPerTick` sections per tick and pauses while 256 frames are
  queued; B&S decodes for at most 6 ms and rebuilds at most 4 sections per frame, nearest first.
- **Unknown vs empty:** a section that has not arrived is unknown (faces against it are drawn, and the player is
  held in place at spawn until the sections under them are built); an all-air section arrives as a
  one-entry palette.
- **Lifecycle:** B&S connect -> Hello -> (world loaded) Welcome -> StateDef/Section/Entity streams.
  Minecraft world change or reconnect -> new Welcome -> B&S clears its world and proxies. B&S disconnect ->
  Minecraft gives the keyboard back.
