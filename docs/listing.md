# Melty listing (draft: stays a draft until the build has run in both games)

**Title:** CraftBlade

**Tagline:** Step into your Minecraft world in VR and fight its mobs with Blade & Sorcery's physics combat.

**Description:**
Your Minecraft world, rebuilt around you in Blade & Sorcery. Walk, climb and fall on real Minecraft blocks
(drawn with your own Minecraft textures). Zombies, skeletons, pillagers and your friends show up as
Blade & Sorcery fighters: slash, stab, smash or throw your weapons, and Minecraft takes the hit. They get
knocked back, die and drop as ragdolls. Your friends in Minecraft see you moving, with your arms following
your real hands. Host a world or join a friend's through Melty, up to 8 players.

Needs Minecraft: Java Edition, Blade & Sorcery (PC VR) and a VR headset.

**Games:** Minecraft: Java Edition (primary), Blade & Sorcery (companion)
**Players:** 1-8, online (host + join link)
