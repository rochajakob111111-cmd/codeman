# Third-party software in CraftBlade releases

| Component | Author | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prism Launcher (portable) | Prism Launcher contributors | GPL-3.0 | https://github.com/PrismLauncher/PrismLauncher |
| Fabric API | FabricMC | Apache-2.0 | https://github.com/FabricMC/fabric |
| e4mc | vgskye and contributors | MIT | https://github.com/vgskye/e4mc-minecraft-architectury |
| Fabric Loader (installed by Prism at first start) | FabricMC | Apache-2.0 | https://github.com/FabricMC/fabric-loader |

Minecraft and Blade & Sorcery are not included: every player uses their own copies. Minecraft's textures shown
inside Blade & Sorcery are read live from the player's own Minecraft client; B&S creatures come from the
player's own B&S install.
